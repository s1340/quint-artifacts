What Must Be Built (✦ Novel)

  • Spike-T — tactile encoder with spike-to-token formatting
  • Dual pathway — fast reflex + semantic understanding with confidence gating
  • STDP distillation interface — connect online SNN learning to offline LLM training (highest risk)
  • Tactile MED profile — empirically set T_tact for touch
  • Emotion vector interaction — how touch modulates internal concepts

Three Tokenizers Removed

  • Sensor → Model: FBG → spikes directly. No image. No pixels. No text tokens.
  • Inside Model: SpikeLLM processes spikes natively. Tactile spikes enter as spikes.
  • Experience → Persistence: STDP updates per event. No batched training needed.
  • SSI connection: the same mechanism that lets you feel (STDP) is the mechanism that lets you learn from feeling.
